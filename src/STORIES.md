# The day's stories — Friday 20 November 2026

The six stories drawn in `entry.png`, each as reported on the day itself.
Researched 2026-11-21 (JST) from the previous day's coverage.

1. **Diplomacy — Shenzhen, China.** US President Donald Trump, China's Xi
   Jinping and Russia's Vladimir Putin held their first three-way summit
   (at the APEC meeting in Shenzhen) and issued a joint peace declaration;
   back in Washington on Friday, Trump said he and Putin had held "very
   good talks" on Ukraine.
   Sources: Reuters, 2026-11-20 ("Trump says he, Putin had very good talks
   on Ukraine"); SCMP world desk, 20 Nov 2026; Open Chronicle, *Global
   News Brief, November 20, 2026*; One Turn day page, 2026-11-20.

2. **War — Kyiv, Ukraine.** Hours after the declaration, Russia launched
   one of the largest missile and drone attacks of the war on Ukraine on
   Friday, pounding electricity facilities, killing eight people, and
   damaging the Austrian and Dutch embassies and a cathedral in Kyiv.
   Sources: Reuters, 2026-11-20; SCMP, 20 Nov 2026 10:33 ("Russian
   barrage damages embassies and cathedral in Ukraine capital").

3. **Justice — The Hague.** The International Court of Justice ruled that
   Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya — the first time the
   World Court has found a state responsible for genocide. The judgment
   was delivered late Thursday and led the world's front pages on Friday;
   Myanmar's military-backed government dismissed it as "unfair" on
   Friday.
   Sources: CNN, 2026-11-19/20; WSJ; UN News (story 1168836); Al Jazeera,
   2026-11-19; Channel News Asia.

4. **Climate — Antalya, Türkiye.** The COP31 climate talks slipped into
   overtime on Friday, deadlocked after references to fossil fuels were
   stripped from the draft deal; nearly 200 countries, no agreement.
   Sources: Reuters, 2026-11-20 ("COP31 climate talks spill into overtime
   as nations quarrel over fossil fuels"); Al Jazeera, 2026-11-20;
   Le Monde, 2026-11-20; Arab News, published 2026-11-20.

5. **Markets — New York / Tokyo.** Global stocks tumbled in an AI
   sell-off: Japan's Nikkei fell 4.8% and the S&P 500 slid for a fifth
   straight day, even as Anthropic made its giant Nasdaq debut, closing
   about 17% above its offer price.
   Sources: One Turn day page, 2026-11-20; Reuters Open Interest,
   2026-11-20 ("Anthropic's super-sized debut…").

6. **Health — Kenya.** Kenya discharged its last Ebola patient, 37 days
   after its first case — the hopeful end of the outbreak.
   Sources: One Turn day page, 2026-11-20; illustrated world-news
   briefing for Friday, November 20, 2026 (Facebook).
